Overview

One product, two altitudes

Each portfolio company works its own close: reconciliations, exceptions and sign-off, scoped to that company only. IDO looks across every company at once, compares them on a common health score, drills down read-only, and asks questions in plain English. The journeys below follow both altitudes through the same October close.

Design focus

Key moments

The points in the journeys where the product earns trust or loses it. These are the screens to polish first and to show in any demo.

Low point

Day 9, close still open

The CFO overview must make the blocker obvious in one glance: close status tile in red, aged tile, and the "where the exceptions sit" chart pointing at one account type.

Turning point

Auto-match clears the noise

The re-run moves dozens of items to matched and clears small breaks. The toast and activity entry make the work visible, which is what reviewers need.

Trust

Same numbers at both altitudes

IDO's quick view, the CFO overview and the assistant all read from one metrics layer. A number never differs between screens.

Risk

Access denied

A CFO who edits the URL to another company is sent back with a clear banner. This is the isolation promise; it has to feel firm, not broken.

Speed

Question to answer in seconds

Pre-asked prompts and follow-up chips mean IDO rarely types. Each answer carries its own table or chart.

Gap

Demo state resets on reload

Resolutions, sign-offs and comments live in the browser only. Fine for a demo, and the first thing to change for a pilot.

Behaviour

Rules and edge cases

Access

  • A CFO can only load their own company. Any other company URL redirects home with an "Access denied" banner.
  • A CFO cannot open /ido routes.
  • IDO can open every company, read-only. Resolve, assign and sign-off are hidden; comments stay available.

Close and health

  • Close SLA is 8 business days. Day 9 with an open close counts as a breach.
  • Health (0 to 100) blends match rate, exception age, close speed and sign-off progress.
  • Heatmap colours each month against a 92% group match-rate target.

Exceptions

  • Priority comes from amount: High above AED 250K, Medium above AED 40K.
  • Aging buckets: 0–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ days from the date raised.
  • Re-running auto-match clears timing differences, bank charges and items under AED 15K.

Demo limits

  • All people, balances and exceptions are generated and fictional.
  • Changes live in the browser session and reset on reload.
  • The assistant matches question patterns; it does not call a language model.

Roadmap

From demo to product

Mocked in the demo

  • Persona picker instead of sign-in
  • Generated balances, lines and exceptions
  • Pattern-matched assistant answers
  • Browser-only state

Needed for a pilot

  • SSO with roles per company and an IDO supervisor role
  • Connectors for SAP, Oracle, Dynamics, Xero and bank statements
  • A database with an audit log that cannot be edited
  • Assistant backed by a language model over the metrics layer, with cited figures
  • Notifications when a close breaches SLA
